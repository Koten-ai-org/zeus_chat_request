The first three charts are the comparison. A hit of 1 means that pack called the target API. Rows are the largest Zeus result on that turn. Rounds are how many model turns it used. The pattern to look for is a hit, with rows, in fewer rounds. A missing bar means that side did not run. The HTTP chart counts every Zeus call, including 200, 400, and 500. A call whose status is not 200 wears a badge on that pack cell: orange when Zeus rejected the request, red when Zeus failed the call. The verdict column below is still the target-API rule.   • both hit: both packs met the target.
  • both missed: neither pack met the target.
  • not run: a model call is missing, so the row is left out of the score.
  • Orange: a pack cell ends on HTTP 400 or higher, so the row is re-tested later. The source is LLM when the model sent a request Zeus rejected, Zeus when the service closed or failed the call, and koten_converter (FUTURE) when that hop reports the error. koten_converter is not wired into this suite yet. Orange stays out of better and worse. An earlier HTTP status that is not 200 stays on the cell as a badge and in the HTTP chart. It does not turn the row Orange when the turn ends at 200.
