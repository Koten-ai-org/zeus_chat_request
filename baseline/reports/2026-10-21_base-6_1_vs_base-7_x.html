What changed in the text

base-7.x starts from a copy of base-6.1 and adds the CR-35 method. The analytics system message grew by 1,542 characters. 1,282 of those are the new Plan, then call block: World, Entry, Path, Goal, and a budget of at most 8 calls. The access-path line changed from “prefer cheapest that answers” and “8 steps” to “the cheapest move that still follows the plan” and “one pipeline is one call”.

  • search: one soft phrase, the user's words unchanged, entry limit up to 200, and a second soft phrase goes to pipeline.
  • pipeline: one drill-down is one call; later steps use narrow_to; page at 10 or 20, then project.
  • return: a browse with no hard fact ends on 10 or 20 cards.
  • rail is new (751 characters in the tool JSON). Copy name from the scope brief and pass binds only. No SQL.
  • 13 verbs became 14. 10 of the original tools are unchanged.

What in that text shows up in the score

  • beer-walk-beers-by-country is better. base-6.1 called pipeline, find Country, find Brewery, find Beer, return (2 rounds, 0 rows). base-7.x called find Country, find Beer, find Brewery, get, traverse walk_path (4 rounds, 100 rows).
  • beer-colorado-itinerary is better. base-6.1 called pipeline, find Brewery, project, find Beer, get (4 rounds, 12 rows). base-7.x called pipeline, find Brewery, search fts Brewery, set, project, traverse walk_path, find Beer, return (4 rounds, 50 rows).

Those wins are the questions where base-7.x called traverse walk_path and base-6.1 did not. The new lines that talk about that job are the plan: the world already includes WALK_PATHS, and the path goes from a large set to a smaller set. rail was not called. The search and pipeline rewrites did not change beer-pipeline-cards: both packs still called search fts inside the pipeline. Travel stayed even.
